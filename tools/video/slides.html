Why

Listening beats squinting

Long pages are tiring

Articles, reports and manuals take a lot of screen reading.

Good voices cost money

The natural-sounding readers are often paid subscriptions.

…or send your text away

Cloud voices get every word you listen to.

Neural Voices: natural voices, free, and the text never leaves your computer.
How it works

Select, click, listen

  • Read a selection, the whole article, or from any sentence you click
  • The sentence is shaded, the word is marked
  • Skips menus, footers and footnote marks
Auto-detect

It knows the language

  • English and Swedish, paragraph by paragraph
  • A mixed page switches voice by itself
  • Other languages follow the page's language
Your voices

A few voices you choose

  • Keep a short list in the player
  • A default voice for each language
  • 170 voices in 49 languages to add, each downloaded once
Natural and light

Sounds right, stays out of the way

0.75–2×Same pitch at any speedSpeed is set inside the voice. Measured: 176–181 Hz from slow to fast.
0.75 sTo the first wordWhen the voice is ready. Opening the popup gets it ready.
0%CPU after StopMemory is freed after a few quiet minutes.
