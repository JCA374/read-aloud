Your voices

These show in the player and popup, in this order. A voice downloads once, the first time it's used, or now with Download. It's then kept in the browser and works offline. ▶ Try plays a short sample.

Language

English and Swedish are recognised from the words. Other languages come from what the web page says it's written in.

Storage and settings
